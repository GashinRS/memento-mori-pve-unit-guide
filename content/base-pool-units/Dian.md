Dian has two distinct PvE uses. At the start of battle she increases her own evasion and that of the ally with the highest ATK, making her a cheap defensive support for a carry even when Dian is not dealing meaningful damage herself.

With investment, Dian can instead be used as a direct-damage carry. Her S2 consumes 35% of her maximum HP while she is above 40% HP, then deals direct damage based on the HP consumed. Because the damage scales with maximum HP rather than ordinary ATK multipliers, HP support from Fia is especially valuable.  Enemy DEF does not affect the damage, neither does her own PMDB. 

When Dian falls below 40% HP and at least 1 other ally is alive, she gains Invisibility. This lets her ignore all targets from any attack, buffs and debuffs from enemies and allies alike. This means that as long as that other ally is alive, she can keep doing damage without being killed herself. For this reason, she is often used in Main Quest in combination with Belle, who can become essentially immortal under the right circumstances.

Shiloh can be considered a sidegrade to Merlyn; both units can buff the attack, crit rate and crit damage of 2 allies. Merlyn has the benefit of her crit buff lasting for the entire battle, and Shiloh has the additional benefit of not being tied down to positioning like Merlyn, and also buffing up 2 allies' defense by 150% of Shiloh's own defense. However, Shiloh is unfortunately not as universally useful as Merlyn. One of the main reasons is that Merlyn's permanent crit rate buff is very valuable for a lot of different situations where battles last longer than 4 turns, which tend to be quite common. Shiloh's crit damage buff is also lower in Tower of Infinity at the start of battle, which causes you to lose out on a lot of early damage and subsequently means that she is not very useful in that game mode. 

What all of this means in practice is that Shiloh will mainly be useful in two scenarios, the first one being early to mid-game when you do not have a lot of other supports yet. In this case you can use her in combination with Merlyn. Later down the line she starts to lose value due to limited team slots and Merlyn being the better crit rate/damage buffer in most cases, but Shiloh does still have its niches in certain scenarios where she will be better than Merlyn, especially when a stage requires more bulk. The second use case is in Florence + Cordie teams, where in many situations Shiloh can actually be better than Merlyn due to the additional bulk she provides alongside similar damage buffs. This is very stage dependent though and in most cases Merlyn will still get the job done just fine.

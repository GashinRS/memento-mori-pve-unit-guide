Artoria can increase both the HP and M.DEF of the ally with the highest attack by 30%. She can additionally provide some
extra bulk to your team restoring 80% of damage taken (20% max). The latter effect is quite niche however and only works
while she is alive. One benefit Artoria has over the other units in this section is that she does not need her UW for her full HP support, making her a lot cheaper to use.

# Gear Guide
MementoMori does a poor job of explaining of how gear works, making it easy to invest resources in the wrong places. Properly understanding how you should gear your characters is fundamental game knowledge that is arguably the single most important factor in team building in both PvE and PvP. This guide will cover these fundamentals and provide examples of what you should and not should not do with regard to gear investment. 

## Defense
Before we get into any specifics it is important to establish that the Defense stat is **the most important stat** you can get from your gear, ignoring weapons. Throughout the guide you will notice that there is always a heavy emphasis on Defense, because it is simply that important to have. This is due to Defense being the largest contributor to survivability for your units. In most circumstances this should be the only stat to laser focus on when limited by resources. If there is anything to take away from this guide, it is this. As a side note, Defense is not the same as P.DEF and M.DEF, as Defense plays a bigger role in the damage formula and is this better than both P.DEF and M.DEF by a pretty wide margin.

## Gear Rarities
Gear has several rarities, of which we will cover R, SR, SSR, UR and LR gear. While rarities below R exist, these should all eventually be replaced by R or higher rarity gear as soon as you are able to. 

### R Gear
This is the lowest rarity gear you should consider running. Its main purpose is mostly just to have some gear to put on your characters and will eventually be completely replaced by SR or higher rarity gear, but this process will take time. 

### SR Gear
This is the most cost-efficient gear you can get and will be something you will never fully replace simply due to how good it can be for the cost. The main reason for SR gear being so good is that the Haniel (lvl 240) set bonus grants 30% Defense, which can bring the raw Defense gained from SR gear on par with SSR gear. This in turns means that SR gear is the ideal gear for your support units in most situations. Even Sandalphon (lvl 180) already provides a 10% Defense set bonus at 2 pieces, so this can already be used if you cannot afford Haniel yet. Eventually you will want 3-4 full sets of SR gear for your supports. Focus on the gloves and boots for Defense and keep the rest at lvl 240. 

### SSR Gear
This is where we start getting into premium gear territory. SSR gear is much harder to get than SR gear and will be something you can only afford to put on 1-2 units. It is the gear your main DPS should be using. The most interesting set bonuses SSR gear provides are Lucifugus's (lvl 200) DEF Break at 2 pieces and Satan's (lvl 240) 25% HP and 16% ATK at 2 and 4 pieces respectively. 

### UR and LR Gear
UR and LR gear are discussed together because they fall within the same category of the most premium and best gear you can get. UR gear starts at lvl 240 and cannot be equipped by lower level characters. It can be upgraded to LR using 50 Tree of Life's Dews. LR gear is especially good because the DEF break set bonus at just 2 pieces, where DEF Break is the best offensive stat in the game. Note that LR gear can only be equipped by characters who are at LR5 or higher rarity. 

Because of how hard UR/LR gear is to get and level, the first piece you should always focus on is the boots. The reason for this is as follows: we have already established that Defense is the stat you should focus on. This leaves only gloves and boots as viable first UR/LR options. However, by getting UR/LR boots, you can evenly spread out the usage of your Astaroth scrolls and tomes, since you can get gloves and helmets from the scrolls but the tomes provide necklaces, dresses and boots. If you were to get gloves instead, you will simply run out of Astaroth tomes.

The only exception for when you should not get boots as your first UR/LR piece is when you are already quite high level (~400 LL or higher) and need an immediate damage buff, in which case getting an LR necklace first might be the better option. This way you still get the DEF Break set bonus without killing your Defense stat by equipping lvl 240 LR boots over your max level SSR boots. If you do go this route, DO NOT level up the necklace, try focusing on getting and leveling LR boots immediately afterward. 

#### Relics
UR gear can only be obtained from pulling Relics, which in turn can only be obtained from the Invocation of The Seraph's Prophecy. You get 1 free pull every day on this banner, and get a 20% chance to obtain a single relic after 10 pulls, 40% after 25 pulls and 100% after 50 pulls. The catch is that this counter resets every Monday, meaning that you will only have filled the meter to 7/50 by Sunday. This means that **you should always spend 900 gems on Sunday to have a chance to get a relic**. Relics are a never ending upgrade path, you will never max this out on every piece of gear or every character so **do not skip this** unless you are absolutely certain you need the gems for something else.

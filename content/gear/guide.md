# Gear Guide
MementoMori does a poor job of explaining how gear works, making it easy to invest resources in the wrong places. Properly understanding how you should gear your characters is fundamental game knowledge that is arguably the single most important factor in team building in both PvE and PvP. This guide will cover these fundamentals and provide examples of what you should and should not do with regard to gear investment. 

## Defense
Before we get into any specifics, it is important to establish that the Defense stat is **the most important stat** you can get from your gear, not including weapons. Throughout the guide, you will notice that there is always a heavy emphasis on Defense, because it is simply that important to have. This is due to Defense being the largest contributor to survivability for your units. In most circumstances, this should be the only stat to laser focus on when limited by resources. If there is anything to take away from this guide, it is this. As a side note, Defense is not the same as P.DEF and M.DEF, as Defense plays a bigger role in the damage formula and is thus better than both P.DEF and M.DEF by a pretty wide margin.

## Gear Rarities
Gear has several rarities, of which we will cover R, SR, SSR, UR and LR gear. While rarities below R exist, these should all eventually be replaced by R or higher-rarity gear as soon as you are able to. 

### R Gear
This is the lowest rarity gear you should consider running. It mainly serves as basic gear for your characters and will eventually be completely replaced by SR or higher-rarity gear, but this process will take time. 

### SR Gear
This is the most cost-efficient gear you can get and will be something you will never fully replace simply due to how good it can be for the cost. The main reason for SR gear being so good is that the Haniel (lvl 240) set bonus grants 30% Defense, which can bring the raw Defense gained from SR gear up to par with that gained from SSR gear. This in turn means that SR gear is the ideal gear for your support units in most situations. Even Sandalphon (lvl 180) already provides a 10% Defense set bonus at 2 pieces, so this can already be used if you cannot afford Haniel yet. Eventually, you will want 3-4 full sets of SR gear for your supports. Focus on the gloves and boots for Defense and keep the rest at lvl 240. 

### SSR Gear
This is where we start getting into premium gear territory. SSR gear is much harder to get than SR gear and will be something you can only afford to put on 1-2 units. It is the gear your main DPS should be using. The most interesting set bonuses SSR gear provides are Lucifugus's (lvl 200) DEF Break at 2 pieces and Satan's (lvl 240) 25% HP and 16% ATK at 2 and 4 pieces, respectively. 

### UR and LR Gear
UR and LR gear are discussed together because they fall within the same category of the most premium and best gear you can get. UR gear starts at lvl 240 and cannot be equipped by lower-level characters. It can be upgraded to LR using 50 Tree of Life's Dews. LR gear is especially good because of the DEF Break set bonus at just 2 pieces, as DEF Break is the best offensive stat in the game. Note that LR gear can only be equipped by characters who are at LR5 or higher rarity. 

Because of how hard UR/LR gear is to get and level, the first piece you should always focus on is the boots. The reason for this is as follows: we have already established that Defense is the stat you should focus on. This leaves only gloves and boots as viable first UR/LR options. However, by getting UR/LR boots, you can evenly spread out the usage of your Astaroth scrolls and tomes, since you can get gloves and helmets from the scrolls but the tomes provide necklaces, dresses and boots. If you were to get gloves instead, you would simply run out of Astaroth tomes.

The only exception to getting boots as your first UR/LR piece is when you are already quite high level (~400 LL or higher) and need an immediate damage buff, in which case getting an LR necklace first might be the better option. This way, you still get the DEF Break set bonus without killing your Defense stat by equipping lvl 240 LR boots over your max-level SSR boots. If you do go this route, DO NOT level up the necklace; focus on getting and leveling LR boots immediately afterward. 

#### Relics
UR gear can only be obtained from pulling Relics, which in turn can only be obtained from the Invocation of The Seraph's Prophecy. You get 1 free pull every day on this banner, and get a 20% chance to obtain a single relic after 10 pulls, 40% after 25 pulls and 100% after 50 pulls. The catch is that this counter resets every Monday, meaning that you will only have filled the meter to 7/50 by Sunday. This means that **you should always spend 900 gems on Sunday to have a chance to get a relic**. Relics are a never-ending upgrade path. You will never max this out on every piece of gear or every character, so **do not skip this** unless you are absolutely certain you need the gems for something else. 

## Gear Progression
This section will walk you through what gear you should be aiming for depending on your level. Note that the term Level Link (LL) will be used here to refer to the level of your DPS, who should have the highest level on your team when the Level Link tree has not been unlocked yet. 

### Before 180 LL
These are the earliest stages of the game where you don't really have access to any specific gear yet. For this part, you can just auto-equip whatever the game recommends and focus on upgrading your main DPS's weapon first, followed by their boots and gloves. You likely won't have many spare resources besides that, but if you do, level up your supports' boots and gloves.

### Between 180 and 220 LL

At this point, you will have access to most of the gear from the [Gear Rarities section](#gear-rarities), provided you have cleared 13-22, which unlocks the gear banners. Your first order of business here should be to get your main DPS's Unique Weapon (UW), which you can get by spending 24 Magic Crystals. The main banner that provides these (Invocation of the Witches' Secrets) has one daily free pull and a counter for bonus Magic Crystals that resets every Monday. You can either choose to wait it out until you get enough Magic Crystals from the free pulls, or decide to spend some gems here. Magic Crystals are a very precious resource that you will likely never have enough of, so it is not a bad idea to just spend gems to speed up the process. Try to keep in mind, however, that the optimal way to spend on this banner is to reach the 35-pull bonus by Sunday, as this will provide the highest number of Magic Crystals. Remember to always level up your main DPS's UW whenever you can; a higher-level UW is always better than whatever set bonus you might be losing out on.

After getting your main DPS's UW, you should start getting them for some of your support units. The most important one to get first is [Merlyn's](../../base-pool.html#unit-Merlyn). You can refer to the [Limited PvE Guide](../../index.html#unit-overview) and [Base Pool Guide](../../base-pool.html) to find out which UWs are worth getting and which ones aren't.

At this point, you should also try to get some SSR gear for your main DPS. You might already have some SSR scrolls and tomes from pulling on the Invocation of Chance (IOC) banner, but it is recommended to pull the additional scrolls and tomes you need from the Invocation of The Forbidden until you can kit out your main DPS with a full set of SSR gear except for the boots. The reason why you should skip boots is explained in the [UR and LR Gear section](#ur-and-lr-gear). Once you have this, you should be able to keep your SSR gear leveled through the scrolls and tomes you can get from IOC. 

Once you can afford it, you should also consider pulling a bit on the SR banner (Invocation of The Holy). This is mainly for your supports, as the 2-piece level-180 and, later on, 4-piece level-240 bonuses will provide 10% and 30% Defense, respectively.

### After 240 LL
Not much changes at this point; the main focus is still always on the weapon followed by boots and gloves. The main addition is that you can now equip UR/LR gear. Remember to always do your 3 paid pulls on Sunday on the relics banner as [explained earlier](../../gear.html#relics). The 4-piece SSR + 2-piece UR/LR setup for your main DPS will go a very long way. You could even use this forever if you do not want to invest much into gear, as long as you keep the boots leveled. Instead of providing extra examples, please refer to the [Examples](../../gear.html#examples) at the end of this page for examples of mistakes to avoid.
